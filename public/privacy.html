Privacy Policy

Last updated 9 September 2026

Who we are

Core Spaces Marketing Management is a marketing company registered in Dubai, United Arab Emirates under commercial licence number 1646599. We operate this website at corespaces.info and advertise property on behalf of ourselves and our partners.

This policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, and what you can ask us to do with it.

Information we collect

We collect information you give us directly when you enquire about a property. That usually means:

  • Your name
  • Your mobile number
  • Your email address, if you provide one
  • Your answers to questions about what you are looking for, such as budget range, property type and when you plan to buy

You may give us this information through a form on this website, through a lead form on Facebook or Instagram, through WhatsApp, or by contacting us directly.

When you submit a lead form on Facebook or Instagram, Meta may pre-fill some fields from your profile and may ask you to confirm your mobile number with a one-time code. Meta passes the details you submit to us. Meta's own handling of your data is governed by its privacy policy, not this one.

We also collect basic technical information when you visit this website, such as your browser type, device and the pages you view. This is used to keep the site working and to understand which pages are useful.

Why we use it

We use your information to:

  • Contact you about the property you enquired about, by phone, WhatsApp, SMS or email
  • Send you floor plans, pricing, availability and payment plan details
  • Match you with properties that fit what you told us you are looking for
  • Keep a record of our conversation so a colleague can help you if your consultant is unavailable
  • Improve our advertising and understand which of our ads reach the right people

We do not sell your personal information.

Who we share it with

We share your information only where it is needed to answer your enquiry:

  • Our team. Consultants and managers at Core Spaces who handle your enquiry.
  • Property developers. Where you have asked about a specific project and the developer needs your details to register your interest or process a reservation.
  • Service providers. Companies that run our customer records, email, messaging and advertising systems on our behalf, including Zoho and Meta. They may only use your data to provide those services to us.
  • Authorities. Where we are required to disclose information by law or by a regulator.

Where your data is held

Our systems and some of our service providers store data on servers outside the United Arab Emirates. Where that happens, we take reasonable steps to ensure your information remains protected to a standard comparable to the protection it has here.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry records for as long as we are in contact with you about a property, and for a reasonable period afterwards in case you come back to us or a similar project launches. If you ask us to delete your details, we will do so unless we are required to keep them by law.

Leads collected through Meta forms are downloaded into our own systems and are not left on Meta's platform indefinitely.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to:

  • Tell you what information we hold about you
  • Correct anything that is wrong
  • Delete your details from our records
  • Stop contacting you

Email us at the address below and we will action your request. You can also reply STOP to any message from us and we will remove you from further contact.

Keeping your information safe

We limit access to your details to the people who need them, use reputable providers for our customer records and messaging, and protect our accounts with passwords and access controls. No system is completely secure, but we take reasonable care with what you give us.

Children

This website and our services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle personal information, we will update this page and change the date at the top.
